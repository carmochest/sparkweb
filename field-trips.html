School & community visits

Tailor-made visit packages for schools, nurseries, early learning centres, and community groups looking to enrich children's learning through play.

Book Your Date

Why visit Spark

Three-hour private sessions designed to support curriculum-linked learning through hands-on, exploratory play. Suitable for ages 3+.

Nurseries & Preschools

EYFS: sensory play, early literacy, gross motor skills, and imaginative exploration.

Kindergartens & Early Primary

KS1: deeper thinking, collaboration, problem-solving, early science & art.

Community organisations

International schools too — theme-based units, STEAM inquiry, cross-curricular experiences.

The Field Trip Package

Ages 3+ · 3-hour private session · recommended ratio 1 adult per 4 children · minimum group size 10 children

What you'll get

  • Private museum access — full use of all zones, no public visitors
  • All themed zones: Builders & Makers, Story Studio, Sound Garden, Water Exploration, Art Studio, Rotating Installations
  • Guided support from Spark Play Facilitators
  • Age-appropriate provocations across zones
  • Flexible session flow tailored to your group
  • Café space access on request

Pricing structure

Base package3-hour private session, all play zones, trained facilitators, ages 3+
Adult ratio1 adult per 4 children included; extra adults 200 THB/person
Workshop add-onHands-on workshop from the menu: +300 THB per child
Custom workshopCurriculum-linked session: pricing on request

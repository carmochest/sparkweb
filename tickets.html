Museum Visit Packs

Each visit = 1 child + 1 adult, any day of the week. Savings vs. the weekend door rate.

Not shareable — registered to 1 child and 1 adult, verified by the child's photo on file. Extra adults pay the door rate on the day. Check in with your unique QR code. Remaining visits end once a pack expires.

Membership

Unlimited all-day visits — pays for itself in about 4 visits a month.

  • Unlimited all-day visits, every day the museum is open
  • 10% off workshops, camps & birthday parties
  • 10% off at Okonomi and Sesame Bun · 10% off selected items at Today We Read
  • Borrow up to 2 book titles a week (late return 50 THB/day)
  • Invitations to members-only events

Good to know

  • All tickets, packs and memberships are non-refundable but transferable within the same family.
  • Members and pack holders check in by phone number or unique QR code — no cards, no apps.
  • Parties and field trips must be booked in advance.
  • Tickets are delivered by email with a full tax invoice. Prices include 7% VAT. Pay by credit/debit card or PromptPay QR.

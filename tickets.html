Join Now Renew Membership

Good to know

  • Membership is a recurring monthly subscription with a minimum commitment of 3 consecutive months' payment.
  • Memberships are personal and non-transferable — you'll upload a profile photo at registration, used only to verify your identity at check-in.
  • The museum may verify member ID to ensure registration details are accurate; mismatched details can lead to cancellation without further notice.

Ticket fine print

Rescheduling and validity

You can reschedule your booking up to 2 times — contact us by phone or email (details are on your ticket). If you don't visit on your booked date, the ticket is no longer valid and cannot be reused.

Delivery, confirmation, and tax invoice

Tickets are delivered digitally to your email. After purchase you'll receive a confirmation email with your e-ticket, plus a full tax invoice. Ticket prices include 7% VAT.

Payments

We accept credit and debit cards and PromptPay QR at online checkout.

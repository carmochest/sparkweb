What you'll find inside

Interactive Zones

Play that leads the way

Sensory-rich, open-ended areas that support exploration, creativity, and self-directed learning — from the Water Lab to the Builder's Workshop.

Rotating Exhibitions

New ideas every season

Curated installations that transform everyday ideas into tactile, visual, and narrative experiences — a fresh theme to discover each quarter.

Workshops & Family Programs

Make, build, tell stories

Weekly workshops, seasonal sessions, and collaborative family activities that encourage making, building, storytelling, and inquiry.

A Welcoming Environment

Designed for children and their adults

A safe, inclusive space that supports emotional, cognitive, and social development — everyone plays here.

Our approach

Children learn through movement, imagination, and play. Each visit offers new ways to investigate, create, and connect — and our Play Facilitators are always nearby to support safe, independent exploration.

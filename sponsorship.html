Why collaborate with Spark

Bring immersive ideas to life

Collaborate with artists, designers, and educators on playful, interactive, educational installations.

Inspire open-ended exploration

Connect with families and communities

What we can create together

  • Installations that spark curiosity and creativity
  • Play spaces that are safe, durable, and family-friendly
  • Programs that connect children, parents, and communities through shared exploration

Who this is for

Companies, brands, and creators who share our vision of learning through play, want to make a tangible impact on families and children, and value creativity, curiosity, and imagination as much as we do.

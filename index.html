You can reschedule your booking up to 2 times — contact us by phone or email (details are on your ticket). If you don't visit on your booked date, the ticket is no longer valid and cannot be reused.

Tickets are delivered digitally to your email. After purchase you'll receive a confirmation email with your e-ticket, plus a full tax invoice. Ticket prices include 7% VAT.

Questions, answered

Do I need to buy tickets in advance?

Purchasing tickets online helps you skip the queue — especially on weekends and holidays.

Can I drop my child off and pick them up later?

No. Spark is a shared play experience; children must remain with a supervising adult at all times.

How long can we stay?

Details coming soon — check back shortly or ask us at contact@sparkplaymuseum.com.

Can we bring food or snacks?

No outside food in the museum zone. Meals and drinks are available at the café.

What if we lose something?

Contact the front desk or email contact@sparkplaymuseum.com.

Can adults visit without children?

Adults may enter only when accompanying a child, except during designated programs or events.
